# MarketScope.io — Price Data Accuracy & Reliability Audit

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

This audit investigates and documents all price-data inaccuracies, mapping defects, floating-point drift issues, currency conversion errors, timestamp misattributions, and frontend state inconsistencies across MarketScope.io.

Following strict financial data engineering standards:
- **Zero Fabrication Rule**: No price is ever estimated, interpolated, or fabricated.
- **Provenance Requirement**: Every displayed market quote must retain its originating provider, exchange timestamp, and delivery classification.
- **Non-Misleading Classification**: Stale, delayed, or closed market data must never be presented as live real-time.

## 3. Corrective Strategy & Implementation Plan

All 22 defects are addressed across Phases 1 through 14:
1. **Canonical Price Model**: Exact Decimal arithmetic, provenance, and validation status.
2. **Instrument Identity & Exchange Mapping**: Strict multi-attribute resolution via `universal_instrument_master`.
3. **Price Calculation Service**: Decimal calculations, OHLC validation, and dedicated currency conversion.
4. **Data Delivery Classifications**: Strict separation of `LIVE`, `DELAYED`, `END_OF_DAY`, `SYNTHETIC`, and `STALE`.
5. **Frontend Remediation**: Update `MarketOverviewCard`, `MarketTicker`, `IndexPerformanceChart`, `StocksPage`, and `CompanyPage` to use canonical quotes with locale-aware formatting.
6. **Automated Verification**: Comprehensive test suite verifying all 22 corrections.
