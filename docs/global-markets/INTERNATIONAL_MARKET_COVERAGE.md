# International Market Coverage & Global Benchmarks

## 1. Global Coverage Architecture

MarketScope.io tracks major international financial centres across the Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East to provide Indian researchers with comprehensive macro cross-market intelligence.

### 1.1 Americas
- **United States**: S&P 500 (`^GSPC`), Nasdaq 100 (`^NDX`), Dow Jones Industrial Average (`^DJI`), Russell 2000 (`^RUT`), CBOE VIX (`^VIX`). Megacap technology and industrial corporations across NYSE and NASDAQ.
- **Canada**: S&P/TSX Composite (Toronto Stock Exchange).
- **Latin America**: B3 Ibovespa (Brazil).

### 1.2 Europe
- **United Kingdom**: FTSE 100 (`^FTSE`), FTSE 250, ICE Brent Crude Oil.
- **Germany**: DAX 40 (`^GDAXI`), Deutsche Börse XETRA.
- **France & Netherlands**: CAC 40 (`^FCHI`), Euronext 100, AEX Index.
- **Switzerland**: SMI (Swiss Market Index).

### 1.3 Asia-Pacific & Middle East
- **Japan**: Nikkei 225 (`^N225`), TOPIX (Tokyo Stock Exchange).
- **Hong Kong & China**: Hang Seng Index (`^HSI`), Shanghai Composite (`000001.SS`).
- **Singapore & Korea**: Straits Times Index (STI), KOSPI (Korea Exchange).
- **Australia**: S&P/ASX 200 (Sydney).
- **Middle East**: Tadawul All Share Index (TASI, Saudi Arabia).

## 2. Cross-Market Correlations & Global Transmission
Global financial market movements propagate into domestic Indian markets through clear macroeconomic transmission channels:
1. **US Fed Interest Rate Policy & US 10Y Yields**: Directly impacts Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) equity and debt allocation in India and USD/INR exchange rate volatility.
2. **Global Crude Oil (Brent & WTI)**: India imports over 85% of its crude oil requirements. Every $10/bbl change in Brent crude impacts India's current account deficit by approximately 0.5% of GDP and directly influences domestic inflation.
3. **CBOE VIX vs India VIX**: Volatility spikes in US equity options markets typically precede equity risk-off episodes in emerging markets.
4. **Dollar Index (DXY)**: Dollar strength influences rupee valuation and emerging market currency reserves.
