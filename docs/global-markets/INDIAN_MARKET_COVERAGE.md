# Comprehensive Domestic Indian Market Coverage

## 1. Scope of Indian Market Intelligence

MarketScope.io provides end-to-end coverage across the domestic Indian financial ecosystem:
- **Equities (NSE & BSE)**: NIFTY 50, NIFTY NEXT 50, NIFTY BANK, NIFTY IT, NIFTY MIDCAP 100, SENSEX, BSE 100, BSE 500, and individual bluechip stocks.
- **Derivatives Pulse (NSE F&O)**: Index and stock futures, implied volatility (IV), put-call ratios (PCR), open interest (OI) accumulation, futures basis (premium/discount), and India VIX volatility benchmark.
- **Commodities (MCX & NCDEX)**: Gold, Silver, Crude Oil, Natural Gas, Copper, Aluminium, Zinc, and Agricultural derivatives.
- **Currencies & Rupee Benchmarks**: USD/INR, EUR/INR, GBP/INR, JPY/INR official FBIL/RBI reference rates and exchange currency futures.
- **Sovereign Debt & Fixed Income**: RBI Repo Rate, Call Money Rate, 91-Day T-Bills, 182-Day T-Bills, 364-Day T-Bills, Central Government Securities (G-Secs) yield curve (1Y to 30Y), and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs).
- **Collective Investment Schemes**: Mutual Funds (Direct & Regular plans via AMFI), Index ETFs, Gold ETFs, Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs e.g., Embassy, Brookfield, Mindspace), and Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs e.g., Powergrid InvIT).

---

## 2. Market Timings & Settlement Cycles
- **Equities & ETFs**: Regular trading 09:15 to 15:30 IST. Rolling settlement cycle on **T+1 basis** across all NSE and BSE scrips. Optional T+0 instantaneous settlement operational on select scrips.
- **Equity Derivatives (F&O)**: 09:15 to 15:30 IST. Monthly contract expiry on the last Thursday of each expiry month (NIFTY weekly contracts expiry on Thursdays).
- **Commodities (MCX)**: Non-agri trading from 09:00 to 23:30 IST (23:55 during US Daylight Saving Time). Daily mark-to-market settlement in INR.
- **G-Secs & RBI Retail Direct**: Yield discovery via NDS-OM platform from 09:00 to 17:00 IST.

## 3. SEBI Risk Disclosures & Transparency
Under SEBI guidelines for financial analytics platforms:
- Risk disclosures on equity and commodity derivatives prominently note that **9 out of 10 individual traders in the equity F&O segment incur net financial losses**.
- No automated algorithmic trading, buy/sell signal generation, or stock advisory execution is performed. MarketScope is strictly an informational and quantitative research tool.
