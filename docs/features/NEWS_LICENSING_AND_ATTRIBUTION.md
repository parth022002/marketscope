# News Licensing & Attribution Framework Specification

## 1. Compliance Principles & Intellectual Property Guardrails
MarketScope.io operates strictly within legitimate data licensing, open data mandates, public domain disclosures, and fair use quotation principles:
1. **Primary Regulatory Filings**: Filings submitted by listed companies to official regulatory depositories (SEBI, RBI, NSE, BSE, US SEC EDGAR, UK Companies House) are statutory public disclosures. MarketScope ingests, parses, indices, and surfaces these filings as official primary source evidence.
2. **Third-Party Journalism Attribution**: For all journalistic news articles and media releases, MarketScope surfaces concise summary headlines and excerpts strictly accompanied by:
   - Mandatory canonical publisher attribution.
   - Clickable outbound link directly to the original publisher article URL.
   - Verified original publication timestamp and timezone.
   - No reproduction of full paywalled or copyrighted commercial articles without authorized API licensing agreements.
3. **Cryptographic Lineage Tracking**: Every evidence document stores a SHA-256 document hash to ensure cryptographic tamper resistance and provenance verification.
