# MarketScope.io — Fundamental Analysis Methodology
## 5-Pillar Company Scorecard, Three-Statement Model & Shareholding Intelligence

---

## 1. Overview & Tickertape Benchmark

MarketScope.io provides deep equity fundamental research modeled after the institutional clarity of Tickertape and Screener.in:
- **Comprehensive Company Dossier**: Dynamic multi-tab workspace at `/company/[symbol]` providing overview, 3-panel charting terminal, 5-year standardized financial statements, peer relative ranking, and quarterly shareholding distribution.
- **Traceable Financial Facts**: Every metric is linked to canonical filing dates, units, and sources without synthetic inflation.

---

## 2. The 5-Pillar Investment Scorecard

Each evaluated company is scored across 5 fundamental pillars:

| Pillar | Evaluation Metrics | Scoring Methodology |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Valuation** | P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, Historical Percentile | High score if trading at discount relative to 5-year median and industry peers. |
| **Profitability** | Return on Equity (ROE), Return on Capital Employed (ROCE), Operating Margin | High score if ROE > 15%, ROCE > 18%, and margins are resilient across economic cycles. |
| **Growth** | 3-Year & 5-Year Revenue CAGR, Net Profit CAGR, EPS Growth | High score if compounding top-line > 12% and net earnings > 15% annually. |
| **Financial Health** | Debt-to-Equity, Interest Coverage Ratio, Current Ratio | High score if D/E < 0.5, Interest Coverage > 5.0x, and healthy working capital buffer. |
| **Red Flags** | Auditor qualifications, Promoter pledge %, ASM/GSM regulatory watch | High score if zero pledged shares and clean statutory audit opinions. |

---

## 3. Three-Statement Financial Modeling

Standardized 5-year financial statements are structured into:
1. **Income Statement**: Revenue from Operations, Operating Expenses, Operating Profit (EBITDA), Depreciation, EBIT, Interest Expense, Profit Before Tax (PBT), Net Income (PAT), Diluted EPS.
2. **Balance Sheet**: Total Equity Capital, Reserves & Surplus, Total Net Worth, Long-Term Borrowings, Current Liabilities, Property Plant & Equipment (Net Block), Capital Work-in-Progress, Cash & Cash Equivalents, Total Assets.
3. **Cash Flow Statement**: Cash Flow from Operating Activities (CFO), Capital Expenditure (Capex), Free Cash Flow (FCF = CFO - Capex), Cash Flow from Investing Activities, Cash Flow from Financing Activities, Net Cash Change.

---

## 4. Shareholding Pattern Tracking

Tracks institutional participation across consecutive quarters:
- **Promoters**: Insiders and founding group holdings, tracking pledge percentage trends.
- **Foreign Institutional Investors (FII)**: Foreign portfolio capital inflows and outflows.
- **Domestic Institutional Investors (DII)**: Mutual funds, insurance companies, and pension funds.
- **Public & Retail**: Non-institutional and individual retail shareholder base.
