# MarketScope.io — Valuation Methodology & Relative Multiples
## Mathematical Valuation Framework for Multi-Exchange Securities

---

## 1. Multiple Definitions & Formulas

### 1.1 Price-to-Earnings Ratio (P/E)
$$\text{P/E} = \frac{\text{Current Market Price per Share}}{\text{Diluted Earnings Per Share (TTM)}}$$
Where TTM EPS is the sum of diluted EPS across the trailing 4 reported fiscal quarters.

### 1.2 Price-to-Book Ratio (P/B)
$$\text{P/B} = \frac{\text{Current Market Price per Share}}{\text{Book Value per Share}}$$
Where $\text{Book Value per Share} = \frac{\text{Total Equity} - \text{Intangible Assets}}{\text{Total Shares Outstanding}}$.

### 1.3 Enterprise Value to EBITDA (EV/EBITDA)
$$\text{EV} = \text{Market Capitalization} + \text{Total Debt} + \text{Minority Interest} + \text{Preferred Stock} - \text{Cash \& Cash Equivalents}$$
$$\text{EV/EBITDA} = \frac{\text{Enterprise Value}}{\text{Operating Profit Before Depreciation \& Amortization}}$$

### 1.4 Dividend Yield
$$\text{Dividend Yield (\%)} = \frac{\text{Annual Dividend per Share}}{\text{Current Market Price}} \times 100$$

---

## 2. Sector-Relative Normalization

Because multiples vary significantly across industries (e.g. IT services vs Capital Goods vs Banking):
- Price-to-Book and Return on Assets (ROA) are prioritized for Financial Institutions & Banks.
- EV/EBITDA and Free Cash Flow Yield are prioritized for Manufacturing and Infrastructure.
- P/E and Revenue Multiple are prioritized for Technology and Consumer sectors.
- Peer rankings display sector percentile distributions ($0\%$ to $100\%$).
