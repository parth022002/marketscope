# MarketScope.io — Data Rights & Compliance Policy

## 1. Compliance Statement
MarketScope.io respects intellectual property rights, data redistribution policies, and terms of service of all stock exchanges, index sponsors, and commercial market data vendors.

## 2. Redistribution Rules
- **Non-Display Research**: Market data consumed by MarketScope.io is utilized exclusively for calculation, research, educational visualization, and charting.
- **Attribution**: Quotes and charts prominently display provider attribution and exchange identifiers.
- **Delay Notices**: Where real-time commercial redistribution licenses are not active, quotes are visibly tagged with a minimum 15-minute delay indicator (`DELAYED_15M`) in compliance with NSE, BSE, and US exchange regulations.
