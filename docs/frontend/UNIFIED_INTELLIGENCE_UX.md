# MarketScope.io — Unified Intelligence UX Specification

## 1. Concept: MarketScope Unified Intelligence™
MarketScope Unified Intelligence eliminates fragmented, disjointed AI widgets by fusing 8 analytical dimensions into a single research environment (`/intelligence`).

---

## 2. Integrated Analytical Dimensions

1. **Fundamental Intelligence**:
   - Profitability (ROE, ROCE, Operating Margin), Financial Health (Debt/Equity, Interest Coverage), Cash-Flow Quality, Accrual Ratio.
2. **Valuation Intelligence**:
   - Trading Multiples (P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B), 3-Scenario DCF (Conservative, Base, Optimistic), WACC Sensitivity Matrix.
3. **Technical & Momentum Intelligence**:
   - Multi-timeframe trend alignment, RSI Wilder bounds, Supertrend direction, ADX trend strength, EMA ribbon.
4. **Quantitative Factor Intelligence**:
   - Multi-factor model scores (Quality, Value, Momentum, Growth, Low Volatility) powered by Q-Intelligence.
5. **Risk Modeling & Stress Scenarios**:
   - Parametric & Historical Value at Risk (95% 1-day VaR), Expected Shortfall (CVaR), Beta, Simulated Macro Stress Tests.
6. **Sentiment & Event Intelligence**:
   - Fact-checked news analysis, 4-tier publisher credibility, recency time decay, conflicting reports identification.
7. **Market Regime Intelligence**:
   - Hidden Markov 5-state market classification (Bull Steady, Bull Volatile, Bear Volatile, Sideways, Transitional).
8. **Anomaly Detection**:
   - Accrual divergence, unusual volume spikes, price-volume detachment.

---

## 3. Strict Guardrails
- **Score vs. Confidence**: Analytical score (0-100) and Confidence assessment (%) are strictly separated. Confidence represents regulatory evidence coverage, not probability of stock appreciation.
- **Explainability**: Conclusions provide expandable evidence drawers detailing raw data points, formulas, and primary sources.
- **Zero Hallucinated Recommendations**: No simplistic or illegal "Strong Buy" / "Guaranteed Multi-bagger" labels.
