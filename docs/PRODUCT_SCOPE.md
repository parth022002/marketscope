# Product scope — MarketScope.io

## Product definition
MarketScope.io is an India-first financial research and intelligence workspace. It helps users discover listed companies, inspect financial information, understand analytical metrics, review source evidence, compare peers and preserve dated research.

## MVP boundary
- Curated Indian listed common-equity universe.
- Company/instrument search and canonical identity resolution.
- Company dossier and financial statement views.
- Deterministic, documented metrics.
- Source provenance, freshness and data-quality labels.
- Screener, peer comparison, watchlists, notes and immutable research snapshots as staged capabilities.
- Synthetic development fixtures until licensed data is approved.

## Exclusions
- Trading, order placement, broker execution APIs and custody.
- Customer funds, broker credentials or mandatory PAN collection.
- Automated Buy/Sell/Hold instructions or guaranteed prediction claims.
- Unlicensed scraping or redistribution.
- Broad global multi-asset coverage in the first release.
- AI-generated conclusions in the initial vertical slice.

## Product principles
1. Evidence before interpretation.
2. Every important fact should carry a period, unit, source and freshness state.
3. Distinguish issuer, instrument, listing and exchange identity.
4. Distinguish synthetic, licensed, stale, missing and conflicting data.
5. Keep calculations deterministic and versioned.
6. Preserve counter-evidence and research history.
7. Make limitations visible; never imply real-time coverage when data is delayed or EOD.
