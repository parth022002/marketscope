# Fundamental Analysis Engine Specification
**Platform:** MarketScope.io  

## 1. 15 Audited Financial Ratios
1. **Return on Equity (ROE)**: $\frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Shareholders' Equity}}$
2. **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)**: $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Total Capital Employed}} = \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Total Equity} + \text{Total Debt} - \text{Cash}}$
3. **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)**: $\frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Invested Capital}}$
4. **Operating Profit Margin (OPM)**: $\frac{\text{EBITDA}}{\text{Revenue from Operations}}$
5. **Net Profit Margin (NPM)**: $\frac{\text{PAT}}{\text{Revenue from Operations}}$
6. **Debt-to-Equity (D/E)**: $\frac{\text{Total Debt}}{\text{Total Shareholders' Equity}}$
7. **Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR)**: $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Finance Costs}}$
8. **Current Ratio**: $\frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$
9. **Quick Ratio**: $\frac{\text{Cash} + \text{Liquid Inv} + \text{Receivables}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$
10. **Asset Turnover**: $\frac{\text{Revenue}}{\text{Average Total Assets}}$
11. **Free Cash Flow (FCF)**: $\text{CFO} - \text{CapEx}$
12. **FCF Conversion Ratio**: $\frac{\text{Free Cash Flow}}{\text{Net Profit (PAT)}}$
13. **Trailing P/E**: $\frac{\text{Market Capitalization}}{\text{Trailing 12M Net Profit}}$
14. **Price-to-Book (P/B)**: $\frac{\text{Market Capitalization}}{\text{Total Shareholders' Equity}}$
15. **EV/EBITDA**: $\frac{\text{Market Cap} + \text{Total Debt} - \text{Cash}}{\text{EBITDA}}$
