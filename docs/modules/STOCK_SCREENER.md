# MarketScope.io — Universal Stock Screener Module

## 1. Overview
The Universal Stock Screener allows multi-factor filtering across all exchange-listed instruments based on valuation, growth, momentum, technical indicators, and market capitalization.

## 2. Capabilities
- **Prebuilt Thematic Screens**:
  - *Quality Compounders*: ROE > 20%, Debt/Equity < 0.5, 3Y Profit CAGR > 15%.
  - *Undervalued Dividend Payers*: Dividend Yield > 3%, P/E < 15, P/B < 2.0.
  - *52-Week High Breakouts*: Trading within 3% of 52-week high with volume surge.
  - *Deep Value Opportunities*: P/E < 10, P/B < 1.0, positive operating cash flow.
  - *RSI Oversold Bounce Candidates*: 14-period RSI < 35 with positive quarterly profit.
- **Custom Matrix Filters**: Range sliders for Market Cap, P/E, P/B, Dividend Yield, RSI, and Price Distance from 52-Week High.
- **Export**: One-click CSV export of filtered candidate lists (`exportToCsv`).
