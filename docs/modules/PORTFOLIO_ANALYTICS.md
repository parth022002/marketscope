# MarketScope.io — Portfolio Analytics Module

## 1. Overview
The Portfolio Analytics module enables users to track manual holdings, analyze asset allocation across sectors and market caps, evaluate performance versus benchmark indices, and monitor upcoming dividends.

## 2. Analytical Capabilities
- **Asset Allocation**: Breakdown of equity holdings by sector (Financials, IT, Energy, etc.) and market capitalization (Large Cap, Mid Cap, Small Cap).
- **Performance vs Benchmark**: Time-weighted return calculation compared directly against NIFTY 50 and S&P 500.
- **Dividend Calendar**: Aggregation of declared dividends, ex-dividend dates, and estimated cash inflows for portfolio holdings.
