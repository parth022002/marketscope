# MarketScope.io — Regulatory Review & Non-Advisory Compliance Register

## 1. Non-Custodial & Non-Advisory Legal Boundary
MarketScope.io is designed, marketed, and operated strictly as a financial data research, quantitative visualization, and market intelligence platform.
- **No Custody of Funds**: MarketScope.io does not hold fiat currency, cryptocurrencies, or securities.
- **No Brokerage Execution**: MarketScope.io does not route, execute, clear, or settle orders.
- **No Investment Advice**: All analytics, Q-Scores, regime classifications, and AI dossiers are generated by algorithmic models for research purposes and do not constitute personalized financial advice.
