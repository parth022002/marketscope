# MarketScope.io — Trust & Transparency Disclosures

## 1. Core Commitment to Truth
MarketScope.io operates on a fundamental guarantee: **Truth in Market Data**. We reject synthetic or hallucinated financial numbers. If accurate data is unavailable, we explicitly state that data is unavailable or delayed.

## 2. Regulatory & Educational Disclosures
- MarketScope.io is an educational research and financial data visualization platform.
- It is NOT a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA), nor an SEC-registered broker-dealer.
- Technical indicators, scores, and backtest results do not constitute buy/sell recommendations. Past performance simulated in backtests is no guarantee of future returns.
