# MarketScope.io — Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Collection & Usage
- **Account Identity**: Handled securely via Clerk. MarketScope.io stores only `user_id`, email, and user preferences.
- **Analytics & Watchlists**: Watchlists, alerts, and paper trading portfolios are strictly user-private and never shared with third parties, brokers, or advertisers.
- **No Financial PII**: MarketScope.io never collects bank account numbers, PAN cards, Demat IDs, or credit card details.

## 2. Cookie & Local Storage Policy
- Local storage is used exclusively for client performance (caching chart layout preferences, indicator configurations, and UI theme choice).
