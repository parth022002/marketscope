## 2. Licensing Compliance Statement
MarketScope adopts **`lightweight-charts` v5.3.0**, published by TradingView under the permissive **Apache License, Version 2.0**.
- **Commercial Use**: Freely permitted in commercial SaaS products without revenue sharing.
- **Modification & Distribution**: Permitted without restriction, provided copyright notices are retained.
- **No Watermark Requirement**: Unlike proprietary charting widgets, Apache-2.0 requires no forced external logos or third-party tracking scripts.
