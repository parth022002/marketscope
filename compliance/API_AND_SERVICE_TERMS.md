# MarketScope.io — External API & Cloud Service Terms Review

This document summarizes the terms of service, commercial usage permissions, and privacy obligations for third-party cloud services integrated with MarketScope.io.

---

## 1. Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting Services

### Render (Web Service & Container Hosting)
- **Official Terms**: https://render.com/terms
- **Service Type**: Cloud Application Platform (PaaS)
- **Commercial Use**: Fully permitted under Standard/Professional plans.
- **Data Protection**: SOC 2 Type II certified. Meets hosting security standards.

### Neon (Serverless PostgreSQL Database)
- **Official Terms**: https://neon.tech/terms-of-service
- **Service Type**: Cloud Managed Serverless PostgreSQL
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted under standard paid and free development tiers.
- **Data Security**: Encrypted in transit (SSL enforced via `sslmode=require`) and at rest.

---

## 2. Authentication & Identity Management

### Clerk (Authentication & User Management)
- **Official Terms**: https://clerk.com/terms
- **Service Type**: Identity-as-a-Service (IDaaS)
- **Commercial Use**: Fully permitted.
- **Privacy & Compliance**: GDPR and SOC 2 Type II compliant. Integrates cleanly with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

---

## 3. Telemetry & Observability

### Sentry (Error Telemetry & Monitoring)
- **Official Terms**: https://sentry.io/terms/
- **SDK License**: MIT License (`sentry-sdk`)
- **Commercial Use**: Permitted.
- **Privacy Safeguards**: PII scrubbing configured; no passwords or confidential financial credentials transmitted.
